# 🎮 [CRAZY-SUPERMARKET]

CRAZY-SUPERMARKET is a game about the bizarre adventures of a child who gets trapped at a supermarket after hitting a granny.

---

## 📖 Description

- In a strange supermarket with even stranger rules, a boy becomes trapped inside and must fight through different sections such as food, electronics, and school supplies to uncover the mystery of the supermarket and find a way out.
- Talk to NPCs to find out what’s going on, complete their quests to unlock better attacks and items that will make your battles easier. Have fun and get crazy.
- Uncover the mystery of the supermarket and find a way out
